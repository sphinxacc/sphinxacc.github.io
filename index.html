Sphinxery101

Roblox scripter. Discord: @Sphinxery101

Destructible City

Buildings break apart with a rocket launcher and C4. If you take out the bottom floors the whole building collapses. The rubble runs on the client so the server doesn't lag.

Drag Racing

Race an AI on a quarter mile. Launch on green, shift at the right time and use nitrous. You can upgrade and paint your car in the garage. The server checks race times so you can't cheat.

Building System

Place, rotate, move and sell furniture on your own plot. It snaps to a grid (2, 1 or 0.5 studs) and your build saves.

Pathfinding AI

A monster that patrols, chases you through a maze and parkour, and opens doors.

FPS Gun

First person gun with animations and instant hit detection checked by the server.

Pet Hatching

Press E to hatch an egg (80% common, 20% rare), equip pets and claim daily rewards. Data saves.
